Frequently asked questions

Key facts about Quality Pharmaceuticals and how to buy our Ayurvedic products.

Is Quality Pharmaceuticals a licensed and certified manufacturer?

Yes. Quality Pharmaceuticals is a licensed, GMP-certified Ayurvedic medicine manufacturer operating since 1967. Our Manufacturing Licence is A-73/1977/2018 and our GSTIN is 09AAAFQ7540F1Z9.

Where is Quality Pharmaceuticals located?

We are based at Adarsh Nagar, Jajmau, Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh 208010, India, and supply customers and distribution partners across India.

What types of products does Quality Pharmaceuticals make?

We produce a focused range of proprietary Ayurvedic medicines, including syrups, tonics, tablets, capsules, oils, ointments, drops, gripe water, and churan, across categories such as Women's Wellness, Digestive Health, Baby & Family, Liver & Tonics, Cough & Allergy, and Pain & Joint.

Are the products Ayurvedic?

Yes. All our products are proprietary Ayurvedic formulations made with carefully selected herbal ingredients. Product information is provided for awareness and enquiry; use as directed on the label or by a qualified healthcare professional.

How can I buy or order Quality Pharmaceuticals products?

You can enquire about availability, pricing, and ordering through our contact page, or call +91 73553 61236 or +91 99354 30450. We serve both individual customers and distributors.

Do you supply wholesalers and distributors?

Yes. We work with retailers and distribution partners across India and welcome business and distribution enquiries through our contact page.

In which languages is product information available?

Product information is available in English and Hindi, including indications presented in both languages on our product pages.
