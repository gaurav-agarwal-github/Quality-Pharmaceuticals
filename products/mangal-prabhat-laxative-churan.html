Mangal Prabhat (Laxative Churan)

Mangal Prabhat Churn is a time-tested Ayurvedic remedy formulated to combat chronic constipation, hyperacidity, and heartburn. This GMP-certified, Saunf-flavored churan promotes healthy bowel movements and regulates acid levels.

Suitable forAll ages - children and adults
Suggested doseTake 5 g of powder with water at bedtime or late at night.
